# Alpha-1 — Phase Roadmap

This document describes the **role of Alpha-1 within the overall evolution of ConsuMaarg**.

It clarifies what Alpha-1 enables, what follows logically from it, and what conditions must hold before moving forward.

Alpha-1 is a **frozen reference point**.

---

## Role of Alpha-1 in the Product Lifecycle

Alpha-1 defines the **minimum viable foundation** for the system.

It establishes that:

- the system structure is coherent,
- information can flow end-to-end,
- boundaries are enforceable,
- and behavior remains explicit and auditable.

No later phase bypasses Alpha-1.

---

## What Alpha-1 Unlocks

Completion of Alpha-1 unlocks the ability to:

- introduce controlled intelligence within clear boundaries
- accept real-world inputs without architectural risk
- expose the system to limited public interaction
- evaluate usefulness without committing to scale or automation

These capabilities are **structural**, not operational.

---

## What Alpha-1 Does Not Unlock

Alpha-1 does not unlock:

- production readiness
- automation by default
- unrestricted data ingestion
- performance or reliability guarantees
- user growth or engagement metrics

These remain explicitly out of scope.

---

## Conditions to Progress Beyond Alpha-1

Movement beyond Alpha-1 requires that:

- architectural boundaries remain intact
- system behavior remains explainable
- limitations are clearly communicated
- added capability does not introduce hidden coupling

Progression is gated by **evidence**, not timelines.

---

## Relationship to the Global Roadmap

This phase-local roadmap complements the global product roadmap.

- The global roadmap describes _direction_
- This document describes _readiness_

Alpha-1 provides the baseline against which future phases are evaluated.

---

## Permanence of This Roadmap

This document reflects Alpha-1 as a completed and frozen phase.

- It does not evolve with later phases
- It is not retroactively updated
- It exists to anchor interpretation of future progress

Alpha-1 defines what must remain true as the system grows.

# Alpha-1 — Limitations & Non-Guarantees

This document defines the **explicit boundaries** of Alpha-1.

It clarifies what Alpha-1 does **not** claim, guarantee, or attempt, so that the phase is interpreted correctly and responsibly.

These limitations are intentional.

---

## Scope of Limitations

Alpha-1 establishes structural viability and conceptual coherence.  
It does **not** establish operational maturity or real-world effectiveness.

Any interpretation beyond this scope is incorrect.

---

## No Outcome Guarantees

Alpha-1 does not guarantee:

- resolution of after-sales issues
- successful escalation outcomes
- responsiveness of brands or service providers
- accuracy or completeness of external information

The system provides **guidance**, not results.

---

## No Automation or Autonomy

Alpha-1 does not include:

- autonomous decision-making
- background processing
- automated follow-ups
- system-initiated actions

All system behavior is explicit and user-initiated.

---

## No Intelligence Claims

Alpha-1 does not claim:

- reasoning depth
- predictive capability
- learning or adaptation
- confidence scoring or ranking

Any intelligence-like behavior in Alpha-1 is **bounded, conservative, and deterministic**.

---

## No Real-Time or Live Coverage

Alpha-1 does not guarantee:

- real-time updates
- live monitoring of issues
- comprehensive market or brand coverage

Information availability is limited and explicitly scoped.

---

## No User Personalization

Alpha-1 does not include:

- user profiles
- historical memory
- personalized recommendations
- adaptive behavior across sessions

All guidance is context-based, not user-specific.

---

## No Legal or Advisory Authority

Alpha-1 does not function as:

- legal advice
- financial advice
- consumer court guidance
- an official intermediary or representative

Users remain fully responsible for their decisions and actions.

---

## No Performance or Scale Guarantees

Alpha-1 does not claim:

- high availability
- low latency
- scalability under load
- production-grade reliability

Performance characteristics are out of scope.

---

## Why These Limitations Exist

These limitations exist to ensure that:

- system behavior remains explainable
- expectations remain realistic
- risk is controlled
- future capability is introduced deliberately

Alpha-1 prioritizes **clarity over capability**.

---

## Relationship to Future Phases

Later phases may relax or address some of these limitations.

However:

- no limitation is removed implicitly
- capability expansion requires explicit validation
- Alpha-1 remains the baseline reference

Limitations define responsibility as much as features do.

---

## Closing Note

Alpha-1 is complete within its intended boundaries.

Understanding what Alpha-1 does **not** do is essential to understanding what it successfully establishes.

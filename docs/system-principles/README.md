# System Principles

ConsuMaarg is designed around a small set of principles that guide how the system behaves, what it attempts to do, and what it intentionally avoids.

These principles are not implementation details.  
They describe **how the system relates to users, information, and uncertainty**.

They apply regardless of phase, feature set, or level of automation.

---

## Core Orientation

ConsuMaarg exists to provide **clarity**, not authority.

The system is designed to:

- reduce confusion around after-sales issues
- present structured, evidence-based guidance
- preserve uncertainty where information is incomplete

It does not exist to:

- enforce outcomes
- replace official support channels
- act on behalf of users

---

## Principle Categories

The system principles fall into three broad categories:

- **Boundaries** — what the system does and does not do
- **Safety** — how harm and misuse are avoided
- **Integrity** — how correctness is preserved under uncertainty

Each category is described in the documents that follow.

---

## Why Principles Matter

After-sales issues often involve:

- fragmented responsibility
- conflicting information
- high emotional and financial stakes

In such environments, overconfidence causes harm.

These principles exist to ensure that:

- the system remains trustworthy
- guidance remains realistic
- users retain agency over their decisions

ConsuMaarg prioritizes **being careful over being impressive**.

# Ethics & Safety

ConsuMaarg is designed with the assumption that **misleading guidance can cause harm**, even when intent is good.

This document outlines the ethical and safety posture of the system.

---

## Core Safety Commitments

The system commits to:

- avoiding overconfidence
- preserving uncertainty
- refusing to speculate when data is insufficient
- favoring silence over hallucination

Being incomplete is acceptable.  
Being misleading is not.

---

## Handling Uncertainty

When information is unclear or conflicting, the system may state:

- “Evidence is mixed”
- “Data is insufficient”
- “This appears uncommon”

These are valid and correct outcomes.

The system does not force conclusions where none exist.

---

## Avoiding Harmful Automation

The system avoids:

- autonomous decision-making
- irreversible actions
- hidden background behavior
- persuasive or manipulative outputs

Any automation introduced is bounded and transparent.

---

## Respect for Users

ConsuMaarg is built to respect users by:

- not impersonating authority
- not pressuring action
- not exploiting distress or urgency
- not obscuring limitations

Users are treated as decision-makers, not endpoints.

---

## Ethical Scope

ConsuMaarg does not claim moral, legal, or institutional authority.

Its ethical responsibility is limited to:

- accuracy of representation
- honesty about uncertainty
- restraint in capability

Anything beyond that belongs to official institutions, not this system.

---

## Closing Note

Ethical systems are defined less by what they can do,  
and more by what they **refuse** to do.

These constraints are deliberate.

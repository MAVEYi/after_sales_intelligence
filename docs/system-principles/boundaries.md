# System Boundaries

This document defines the **explicit boundaries** of ConsuMaarg.

These boundaries are intentional and protective.  
They clarify what the system does, and just as importantly, what it does not do.

---

## What the System Does

ConsuMaarg:

- observes publicly available after-sales issue signals
- identifies recurring patterns and themes
- summarizes information in a structured form
- presents guidance with explicit uncertainty
- remains read-only with respect to user actions

The system supports understanding, not enforcement.

---

## What the System Does Not Do

ConsuMaarg does **not**:

- file complaints on behalf of users
- contact brands, sellers, or service centers
- provide legal, financial, or regulatory advice
- guarantee outcomes or resolutions
- predict success or failure
- rank or score brands or services

Any interpretation beyond this scope is incorrect.

---

## User Responsibility

Users retain full responsibility for:

- deciding whether to act
- choosing escalation paths
- interpreting guidance in their own context

The system provides information, not instructions.

---

## Data Boundaries

The system:

- relies only on publicly visible information
- avoids collecting personal identifiers
- does not retain private communications
- does not track users across sessions by default

These boundaries exist to protect users and reduce risk.

---

## Why Boundaries Are Strict

In after-sales contexts, blurred boundaries lead to:

- false expectations
- misplaced trust
- legal and ethical risk

Clear limits preserve clarity, safety, and credibility.

# Product Vision – After Sales Intelligence

## Problem

After-sales service in India is fragmented across brands, sellers, and service partners.
Consumers lack reliable guidance once the purchase window closes.

## Gap

Existing solutions are:

- Brand-controlled
- Complaint-only (no guidance)
- Reactive rather than analytical

## Vision

Build a neutral intelligence layer that:

- Understands recurring after-sales failure patterns
- Maps realistic escalation paths
- Guides users without pretending to replace legal or consumer courts

## Non-Goals

- Replacing legal advice
- Acting as a complaint platform
- Guaranteeing outcomes
